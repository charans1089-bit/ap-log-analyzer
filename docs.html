🏎️ Part 1: Non-Technical User Guide (What It Does & How to Use)

What is this app?

When you drive a modified or tuned turbo vehicle (such as the 2022+ Subaru WRX with the 2.4L FA24 engine), your COBB AccessPort (AP) records hundreds of engine parameters up to 50 times per second into a .csv spreadsheet. Reviewing thousands of rows manually in Excel is tedious and prone to missing quick knock events or lean air/fuel spikes.

AP Log Analyzer Pro automatically parses your log files right in your browser, evaluates your engine health, spots critical hazards, and lets you chat with Google Gemini AI to diagnose your car's tune.

How to Export Logs from your AccessPort

  1. Plug your COBB AccessPort into your car's OBD-II port.
  2. Perform a standard logging run (e.g., cruising or a 3rd gear Wide Open Throttle pull from 2,500 RPM to 6,200 RPM in a safe environment).
  3. Connect your AccessPort to your PC/Mac using a USB cable.
  4. Open the Accessport Manager software → go to Settings → Datalog → Manage Logs.
  5. Select your log and click Export via USB to save the .csv file to your computer.

How to Use the App

  • Upload Logs: Drag and drop your exported .csv files into the upload box on the home screen.
  • Engine Health Score (0–100): View your instant safety grade. A clean log gets 90–100 (A+). Knock events or lean spikes will drop the score.
  • Tri-Fold Diagnostics:
    • 🟢 The Good: Confirmed safe operating parameters (e.g., DAM locked at 1.000, proper fuel rail pressure).
    • 🟡 The Better: Items to improve (fuel trim drift > ±8%, minor charge air heat soak).
    • 🔴 The Worst: Critical risks requiring immediate attention (DAM drops, high knock retard, lean AFR under boost).
  • Side-by-Side Comparison: Compare two logs (e.g. before vs after a mod or tune revision) to see differences in boost, timing, intake temps, and knock counts.
  • Gemini AI Calibration & Chat: Click "Run Gemini Master Calibration" to stream a professional tuner report, or type questions in the chat box to ask anything about your log.

Component Breakdown

  • parser.js: Ingests raw CSV text, detects delimiters (tab vs comma), strips unit noise, matches 55+ monitor aliases, validates row field counts, and extracts COBB AP metadata.
  • metrics.js: Detects Wide-Open-Throttle (WOT) pull regions where Throttle > 90%, Boost > 0 psi, and RPM ≥ 2,500 over ≥ 0.5s spanning ≥ 500 RPM. Computes peak load, IAT range, and ethanol percent.
  • findings.js: Runs diagnostic rule evaluation against engine safety thresholds (DAM < 1.0, FBK ≤ -4.0°, FKL ≤ -2.0°, AFR > 12.5 under boost, Injector Duty > 90%, Fuel Pressure drops > 15%).
  • ai-analyzer.js: Categorizes findings into Good/Better/Worst, calculates the weighted Engine Safety Score (0–100), synthesizes tuner recommendations, and generates MCP prompts.
  • charts.js: Custom responsive HTML5 Canvas charting engine with a synchronized SharedViewport, hover time-scrubber across all channels, WOT pull region highlights, and Safari roundRect fallbacks.
  • gemini.js: Direct client-to-Google Gemini API service supporting Server-Sent Events (SSE) streaming, context-aware telemetry prompting, multi-turn chat, and side-by-side log comparison reviews.
  • storage.js: Asynchronous IndexedDB storage engine (`APLogAnalyzer`) storing metadata and raw telemetry locally in the browser sandbox.

Privacy & Air-Gap Model

All CSV parsing, rule evaluations, and charting are executed locally inside your browser process. Log data is never stored on an external server. When Gemini AI is activated, telemetry summaries are streamed directly from your browser to Google's Gemini endpoint using your own private API key.

📖 Part 3: Subaru FA24 Metric Reference Dictionary

Below is a reference guide for the key engine channels monitored by this application:

Monitor Channel Ideal / Safe Target Warning / Hazardous Threshold Tuning Significance
Dyn Adv Mult (DAM) 1.000 < 1.000 Global timing multiplier. Drops indicate persistent knock. Address before driving hard.
Feedback Knock (FBK) 0.00° to -1.41° (cruising) ≤ -2.80° (under WOT) Real-time reactive knock correction. Cruise blips are normal; WOT knock is dangerous.
Fine Knock Learn (FKL) 0.00° < -1.40° (during pull) Learned timing retard in specific load/RPM cells. Persistent FKL requires map adjustment.
Air/Fuel Ratio (AFR) 11.2 – 11.8 (under boost) > 12.2 (under boost) Mixture under positive manifold pressure. Lean mixtures risk pre-ignition and piston damage.
Fuel Rail Pressure > 2,000 psi (at WOT) < 1,800 psi (under load) High Pressure Fuel Pump (HPFP) direct injection pressure. Dips indicate fueling limitation.
AF Learning 1 (LTFT) ± 0% to 5% > ± 10% Long-term fuel trim. Large positive values mean vacuum leaks; negative mean rich conditions.
Injector Duty Cycle (IDC) < 80% > 88% Percentage of time fuel injectors are open. >90% indicates fueling system is maxed out.
Intake Air Temp (IAT) Ambient + 10-25°F > 120°F Charge air temp. High IATs reduce oxygen density and drastically increase detonation risk.
Engine Oil Temp 195°F – 225°F > 245°F High temperatures break down oil viscosity and film strength. Install an oil cooler if tracking.
