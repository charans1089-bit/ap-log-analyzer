The Complete AP Log Analyzer Guide

A comprehensive overview of how the analyzer works, how to export and interpret your datalogs, the technical architecture, and how to calibrate your tune with Google Gemini AI.

🏎️ Part 1: User Guide & Complete Walkthrough

What is this app?

When you drive a modified or tuned turbo vehicle (such as the 2022+ Subaru WRX with the 2.4L FA24 engine), your COBB AccessPort (AP) records hundreds of engine parameters up to 50 times per second into a .csv spreadsheet. Reviewing thousands of rows manually in Excel is tedious and prone to missing quick knock events or lean air/fuel spikes.

AP Log Analyzer Pro automatically parses your log files right in your browser, evaluates your engine health, spots critical hazards, and lets you chat with Google Gemini AI to diagnose your car's tune.

How to Export Logs from your AccessPort

  1. Plug your COBB AccessPort into your car's OBD-II port.
  2. Perform a standard logging run (e.g., cruising or a 3rd gear Wide Open Throttle pull from 2,500 RPM to 6,200 RPM in a safe environment).
  3. Connect your AccessPort to your PC/Mac using a USB cable.
  4. Open the Accessport Manager software → go to Settings → Datalog → Manage Logs.
  5. Select your log and click Export via USB to save the .csv file to your computer.

Step-by-Step Feature Walkthrough

  • 1. Upload or Try Demo Log: Drag and drop your .csv files into the upload box on the home screen, browse from your Files app, or tap ⚡ Try Demo Log for instant sample telemetry.
  • 2. Engine Health Score (0–100): View your instant safety grade. A clean log gets 90–100 (A+). Knock events, lean spikes, or fuel pressure dips automatically drop the score.
  • 3. Tri-Fold Diagnostics:
    • 🟢 The Good: Confirmed safe operating parameters (e.g., DAM locked at 1.000, proper fuel rail pressure).
    • 🟡 The Better: Items to improve (fuel trim drift > ±8%, minor charge air heat soak).
    • 🔴 The Worst: Critical risks requiring immediate attention (DAM drops, high knock retard, lean AFR under boost).
  • 4. Synchronized Multi-Channel Graphs:
    • Desktop (Mac / Windows): Hover to scrub all 5 graphs simultaneously. Scroll wheel to zoom in/out around cursor. Drag horizontally to pan.
    • Touch (iPhone / iPad): Drag 1 finger across graphs to scrub channels. Pinch with 2 fingers to zoom. Double-tap or tap ⟲ Reset to reset graph range.
    • 1-Tap WOT Zoom: Click any pull pill in the sidebar to auto-zoom straight to that WOT run.
  • 5. Side-by-Side Comparison: Compare two logs (e.g. before vs after a mod or tune revision) to see differences in boost, timing, intake temps, and knock counts.
  • 6. Gemini AI Calibration & Chat: Connect your Gemini API key to stream live Master Tuner calibration reports and ask follow-up questions about knock events or fuel adjustments.

Component Breakdown

  • parser.js: Ingests raw CSV text, detects delimiters (tab vs comma), strips unit noise, matches 55+ monitor aliases, validates row field counts, and extracts COBB AP metadata.
  • metrics.js: Detects Wide-Open-Throttle (WOT) pull regions where Throttle > 90%, Boost > 0 psi, and RPM ≥ 2,500 over ≥ 0.5s spanning ≥ 500 RPM. Computes peak load, IAT range, and ethanol percent.
  • findings.js: Runs diagnostic rule evaluation against engine safety thresholds (DAM < 1.0, FBK ≤ -4.0°, FKL ≤ -2.0°, AFR > 12.5 under boost, Injector Duty > 90%, Fuel Pressure drops > 15%).
  • ai-analyzer.js: Categorizes findings into Good/Better/Worst, calculates the weighted Engine Safety Score (0–100), synthesizes tuner recommendations, and generates MCP prompts.
  • charts.js: Custom responsive HTML5 Canvas charting engine with a synchronized SharedViewport, hover time-scrubber across all channels, WOT pull region highlights, and Safari roundRect fallbacks.
  • gemini.js: Direct client-to-Google Gemini API service supporting Server-Sent Events (SSE) streaming, context-aware telemetry prompting, multi-turn chat, and side-by-side log comparison reviews.
  • storage.js: Asynchronous IndexedDB storage engine (`APLogAnalyzer`) storing metadata and raw telemetry locally in the browser sandbox.

Privacy & Air-Gap Model

All CSV parsing, rule evaluations, and charting are executed locally inside your browser process. Log data is never stored on an external server. When Gemini AI is activated, telemetry summaries are streamed directly from your browser to Google's Gemini endpoint using your own private API key.

📖 Part 3: Subaru FA24 Metric Reference Dictionary

Below is a reference guide for the key engine channels monitored by this application:

Monitor Channel Ideal / Safe Target Warning / Hazardous Threshold Tuning Significance
Dyn Adv Mult (DAM) 1.000 < 1.000 Global timing multiplier. Drops indicate persistent knock. Address before driving hard.
Feedback Knock (FBK) 0.00° to -1.41° (cruising) ≤ -2.80° (under WOT) Real-time reactive knock correction. Cruise blips are normal; WOT knock is dangerous.
Fine Knock Learn (FKL) 0.00° < -1.40° (during pull) Learned timing retard in specific load/RPM cells. Persistent FKL requires map adjustment.
Air/Fuel Ratio (AFR) 11.2 – 11.8 (under boost) > 12.2 (under boost) Mixture under positive manifold pressure. Lean mixtures risk pre-ignition and piston damage.
Fuel Rail Pressure > 2,000 psi (at WOT) < 1,800 psi (under load) High Pressure Fuel Pump (HPFP) direct injection pressure. Dips indicate fueling limitation.
AF Learning 1 (LTFT) ± 0% to 5% > ± 10% Long-term fuel trim. Large positive values mean vacuum leaks; negative mean rich conditions.
Injector Duty Cycle (IDC) < 80% > 88% Percentage of time fuel injectors are open. >90% indicates fueling system is maxed out.
Intake Air Temp (IAT) Ambient + 10-25°F > 120°F Charge air temp. High IATs reduce oxygen density and drastically increase detonation risk.
Engine Oil Temp 195°F – 225°F > 245°F High temperatures break down oil viscosity and film strength. Install an oil cooler if tracking.

📊 Part 5: Standalone Datalog Report Generator

What is the Report Generator?

The Datalog Report Generator is a fast, standalone single-page tool designed to give you an instant executive summary of your COBB AccessPort CSV datalogs. Designed with an authentic 80s/90s arcade CRT aesthetic, it extracts peak boost, minimum AFR, ignition timing corrections, knock events, and Dynamic Advance Multiplier (DAM) health in milliseconds, outputting a clear calibration health verdict.

It operates completely client-side in your browser, saving reports to local browser storage (IndexedDB) with zero external cloud dependencies.

How to Access

The Report Generator is a completely independent page that does not interfere with the primary telemetry dashboard:

  • Direct URL: report-generator.html
  • Top navigation button: Tap 📊 Report Generator in the documentation header bar or launch it directly from any bookmark.

How to Use (Step-by-Step)

  1. Open the Page: Navigate to report-generator.html.
  2. Upload Your Log: Drag and drop your COBB AccessPort .csv datalog file into the cassette drop zone, or tap 📁 Browse CSV File. You can also test the system instantly with ⚡ Try Clean Demo or ⚡ Try Knock Demo.
  3. Scan & Review: Tap 🕹️ Scan Log to parse telemetry. An arcade CRT summary card will render with key metrics, diagnostic warnings, and an out-of-spec event timeline.
  4. Export Your Report: Choose between 💾 Save as PDF (print-ready tuner calibration sheet), 💾 Save as JSON (full telemetry schema for repo backup), or 📊 Save as CSV (spreadsheet summary row).
  5. Compare & Manage: Access previous runs in the 🗄️ Retro Filing Cabinet or run a side-by-side delta analysis with 📈 Compare.

Verdict Classification System

The analyzer automatically evaluates all telemetry against Subaru FA24 safety rules:

  • 🟢 Good tune: Knock count is 0, DAM events < 3 (DAM remains ≥ 0.95), AFR remains rich under boost (11.0–12.2), and timing corrections are within safe tolerances (> -1.0°).
  • 🟡 Watch knock: Minor knock events detected (FBK between -1.0° and -2.8°), or DAM events ≥ 3. Monitor subsequent logs and verify fuel octane.
  • 🔴 Check tune: Critical engine safety threshold exceeded (e.g. lean AFR > 12.5 under boost, severe timing retard ≤ -1.5°/ -4.0°, or DAM drop below 0.85). Cease hard driving and consult your tuner.

Local Storage (IndexedDB Architecture)

Reports are saved locally in your browser's private IndexedDB database (APReportGeneratorDB):

  • Capacity: Stores up to the last 50 reports. Older reports are automatically pruned when new logs are added.
  • Filing Cabinet: Tap 📚 History to open the retro drawer, search past logs, reload historical reports, or delete single entries.
  • Privacy: No telemetry is uploaded to external web servers.

Export Options

  • 1. Save as PDF: Formatted via a clean print stylesheet. Browser automatically suggests SCRK_Report_[YYYY-MM-DD_HH-MM-SS].pdf for tuner review.
  • 2. Save as JSON: Complete structured report file including metadata, metrics, warnings, and out-of-spec moments for repository backups (report_[timestamp].json).
  • 3. Save as CSV: Exports a 22-column comprehensive spreadsheet row (report_[timestamp].csv) with columns: Timestamp, FileName, TuneMap, DurationSec, TotalRows, PeakBoostPSI, MinAFR, MaxTimingRetardDeg, KnockEvents, DAMEvents, MinDAM, PeakRPM, PeakLoad, MinFuelPressurePSI, MaxOilTempF, MaxCoolantTempF, MaxIAT_F, EthanolPct, PeakIDC_Pct, AFLearn1_Pct, Verdict, Warnings.

GitHub Version Control Backup

  1. Click 💾 Save as JSON to download the telemetry report.
  2. Clone your repository: git clone https://github.com/<your-username>/ap-log-analyzer.git
  3. Copy the JSON report into the /reports/ directory.
  4. Commit and push to maintain a permanent version-controlled tuning diary: 
    git add reports/ && git commit -m "Add datalog health report" && git push

Google Sheets Cloud Integration (Webhook & Copy)

You can connect your personal Google Sheet to auto-log every scanned report into a permanent tuning spreadsheet:

  • 1. Create Google Sheet: Create a sheet and paste the 22 header columns in Row 1.
  • 2. Add Apps Script: Go to Extensions → Apps Script, add the doPost(e) webhook snippet, and deploy as a Web App (Access: Anyone).
  • 3. Connect & Auto-Sync: Paste the deployment URL into the ☁️ Sync to Google Sheets box in the Report Generator and check Enable Cloud Auto-Sync.
  • 4. 1-Click Clipboard: Alternatively, tap 📋 Copy Row Format and press Ctrl+V / Cmd+V directly in your spreadsheet!

Features & Limitations

✨ Features

  • Instant client-side CSV parsing & verdict calculation

  • Local-first privacy with IndexedDB storage
  • Interactive 80s/90s arcade theme with CRT effects & 8-bit audio

⚠️ Limitations

  • IndexedDB storage is per-browser (clearing browser cache clears history)
  • Maximum 50 stored reports in local history
  • Google Sheets sync requires a webhook endpoint
  • Client-side analysis only; no external server database

FAQ & Troubleshooting

  • Why is local storage limited to 50 reports? To maintain optimal browser performance and prevent IndexedDB bloat. Older reports are auto-pruned, so export important runs as JSON or PDF.
  • Can I use the Report Generator on iPhone or Android? Yes, the interface is fully responsive with touch-optimized controls and offline capability.
  • What happens if I clear browser cache? Reports stored in IndexedDB will be erased. Be sure to export your reports as JSON or PDF if you want permanent archives.
  • Does it modify my original CSV file? No, file reading is strictly read-only within the browser sandbox.
